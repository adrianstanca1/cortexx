CX-02Intelligence detail

AI where it saves hours, not where it looks clever.

Every AI flow in Cortexx exists because pricing, chasing, and paperwork are where evenings go to die.

⚡ Price a job in 30 seconds

Type the job the way you'd describe it to a mate. Cortexx returns line items with quantities and current UK rates, editable before it becomes a quote PDF.

> "Rip out and refit a 3m × 2.4m bathroom, mid-range, Zone 2"
→ 14 line items generated
Strip-out & disposal ……… £680
First fix plumbing ………… £1,240
Tiling 22 m² ………………… £1,890
Total est. £11,420 · margin 26%

✉ Chase invoices without the awkward email

Overdue invoice? Cortexx drafts the chase — firm but friendly, with the numbers filled in. You review, you send, you get paid.

Draft · INV-0117 · 12 days overdue
"Hi James — quick nudge on invoice INV-0117 for £8,400, due 13 July. Happy to resend the breakdown if useful. Could you confirm payment this week?"
[ Edit ] [ Send ] — 4 s of your time

👁 Photos that file themselves

Point the camera at a receipt — it becomes a categorised expense. Point it at a wall — defects become snags with a location and an assignee.

◆ Vera, your back office

Five AI personas — CEO, CFO, Site Manager, Compliance, Sales — watch your numbers and flag what matters: margin slipping on a job, RAMS about to lapse, a quote going stale.
